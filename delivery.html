Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

  • Processing Time: Orders are packed and processed within 1-2 business days. If you place an order over the weekend or on a holiday, we will process it on the following business day.
  • Delivery Estimates: Once your order is handed over to our courier, it typically takes 2-4 business days to arrive at locations within Metro Manila, and 6-10 business days for provincial locations outside Metro Manila.
  • Order Support: For any questions about your shipping status, please send us a DM on Instagram at @forma_yogamat or reach out through our contact page.

Return Policy

  • Eligibility: We accept returns within 7 days of you receiving your order. Because yoga mats are personal items, the mat must be completely unused, unrolled, and in its original packaging to qualify for a return.
  • Damaged or Incorrect Items: If your mat arrives damaged or you receive the wrong color, please message us within 48 hours with a photo of the item, and we will arrange a replacement immediately.
  • How to Return: To initiate a return, simply DM us on Instagram at @forma_yogamat with your order name and details. Please note that return shipping fees are covered by the customer unless the item arrived defective.
