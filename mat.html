BEST SELLER

Forma Walnut Mat

P 2,500

Color: Walnut

DESCRIPTION

Designed with a moisture-wicking, luxe non-slip PU top, a natural rubber base, and 6mm cushioning, this mat keeps you grounded through every stretch, pose, and flow. Built-in alignment guides and patterns for your hands, knees, and feet help improve posture and form, while an integrated QR code gives you instant access to guided yoga routines and instructions—perfect for both beginners and seasoned practitioners. Crafted for a clean, safe, and durable everyday practice, this non-toxic, PVC-free mat also comes complete with add-ons, including an adjustable carrying strap, box, and a care card to keep your mat in pristine condition.

FIT

  • Size: 183 cm x 61 cm x 6 mm
  • Weight: ~3.5 kg

FABRICATION

  • Top: 100% PU (polyurethane)
  • Bottom: 100% natural rubber
  • Care Instructions:
    • Roll with the top side facing out to prevent creasing.
    • Wipe with a microfiber cloth after use.
    • For cleaning, use a 1:20 oil-free soap or vinegar + warm water solution (apply on cloth, not directly on the mat).
    • Do not machine wash or soak.
    • Avoid lotions, oils, and jewelry before practice.
    • Store away from sharp edges to prevent dents.
