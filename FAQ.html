Frequently Asked Questions

Product & Features

What makes Forma Yoga mats different?
Our mats are designed to help you align with confidence. They feature built-in alignment guides, a moisture-wicking PU top for excellent grip, and a natural rubber base for stability.
What does the QR code on my mat do?
Every Forma mat features an integrated QR code that gives you instant access to exclusive, guided yoga routines.
What colors are available?
The Forma mat currently comes in five signature colors: Walnut (Best Seller), Jet Black, Petal, Pierre, and Emerald.

Orders & Payment

How much is a Forma Yoga mat?
All of our signature mats are priced at ₱2,500.
What payment methods do you accept?
We currently accept GCash, Bank Transfers via MariBank, and Cash payments (available for local pick-up orders only).
Can I use a discount code?
✦ Yes! If you have a promo code (like a pop-up exclusive or student discount), you can apply it directly on the right side of the checkout page before completing your payment.

Shipping & Pick-up

Where do you ship?
✦ We ship nationwide across the Philippines. You can select your specific province and city during the delivery step at checkout.
Do you have a physical store where I can pick up my mat?
✦ While we are an online-first brand, we do offer a "Cash (For Pick-up Only)" option at checkout if you prefer to collect your mat in person.

Brand & Community

Who is behind Forma Yoga?
✦ Forma Yoga started as a thesis requirement by five 4th-year Management students at Ateneo de Manila University and has since evolved into a dedicated guided yoga mat startup.
Do you host physical events or pop-ups?
✦ We do! We regularly host community events and pop-ups. You can check the "Events" tab on our website or follow our "Spotted with Forma" community feed to see where we'll be next.
