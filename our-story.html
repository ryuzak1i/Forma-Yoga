FORMA — GUIDED YOGA MATS

The Mat that gets you

The story of Forma starts in Ateneo de Manila University, where five 4th-year Management students — Ayen Retuta, Isabella Caraan, Jenifer Ochoa, Rae Samson, and Sam Simon — turned a thesis requirement into a vision. Initially, the guided yoga mat was one of several ideas explored for their business thesis project. What began as an academic exercise evolved into something more: a product that combined innovation, functionality, and wellness, with the potential to scale beyond the classroom.

Yoga has always been a central practice for most of the Forma team, but practicing solo sometimes left gaps in alignment and confidence. The idea was simple: could markers on a yoga mat guide practitioners to better postures and form? Testing their own practice confirmed it. The team observed that even subtle guides could improve the way they executed poses. This inspired the tagline, “Align with confidence,” reflecting a mat that empowers practitioners to practice independently without sacrificing proper technique or the benefits of a guided session.
